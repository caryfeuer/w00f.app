Made for groomers

Built with a working groomer who kept her notes on paper, in Apple Notes and in her camera roll, and wanted all of it in one place.

  • A diary, not a salon system. No booking, no payments, no client portal.
  • English and 日本語. Light and dark.
  • Only essential features.

  • Easily recall the last groom

    Open a dog and the last groom is the first thing you see: comb and blade for body, head and ears, how you did the face, and the notes that matter. While you log today's groom, last time stays in view underneath, so you are never working from memory.

  • Log a groom in seconds

    Body, Head and Ears each take a comb and a blade, stepped through real Andis-scale sizes. You choose which sizes appear, so the editor offers only the ones on your table. Face and Notes are free text in any language, and the price starts from the dog's usual rate.

  • Photos capture the details

    Take it with the camera or pick it from your library, up to three per groom. The first photo becomes the dog's cover and stays there, so the shelf of dogs on Home looks like the dogs.

  • Instantly search and find

    Home is shelved by date: Today, Yesterday, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, then by month. Search by name and sloppy typing still finds her. Groomed on Tuesday and wrote it up on Thursday? Set the date and the groom files under the right day.

Your dogs stay yours.

The diary lives on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud. No account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Nobody else can see it, including us. Read the privacy policy.
