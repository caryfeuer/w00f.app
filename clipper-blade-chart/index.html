Snap-on comb numbers aren’t a standard.

The same number can be a different length on another brand’s set. A #5 comb, for example:

What a #5 snap-on comb leaves, by set
Set#5 leaves
Wahl US stainless set (3390-100)1/8″
Andis Universal small set, 9 pieces (12860)3/16″ (5 mm)
Wahl UK competition stainless combs, which number upward (#1 = 3 mm … #8 = 25 mm)16 mm

So go by the length printed on your own comb, not the number.
